You

Heroes

A hero is a lineage plus a class. Lineage sets the body you start with and a slice of extra experience. Class sets the resource you spend, the stat that drives your swings, and the skill tree.

Creation

On the lesson screens you type the four calls in order: a print, a name, a race, a class. In the text campaign you pick a race by number or id (human if you shrug), then 1 warrior, 2 archer, or 3 mage, then a name. A blank name becomes Aldric. Everyone begins at level 1 with 25 gold, two minor health potions, one bread, and six professions at Novice rank, level 1.

Race aliases are accepted: elven, dwarven, ork, beast, dragonborn, and dragon. An unknown race becomes human. An unknown class becomes warrior.

The six attributes

ATK

Damage and how well a weapon sits in your hand. Added straight into every swing.

STR

Health, stamina, carry weight, and the warrior’s hitting stat.

DEF

Incoming damage. It does not subtract a flat amount; it bends the hit.

DEX

Crit chance, crafting precision, and the archer’s hitting stat.

INT

Mana, enchanting, alchemy, and the mage’s hitting stat.

SPD

Dodge, and part of your stamina. Haste and slow change it in a fight.

A human at level 1 is 10 in everything: 250 HP, 200 mana, 220 stamina, 100 carry, 2.5% crit, 2% dodge. A basic swing is ATK plus half of your class stat, and at least 1. For that human, warrior, archer, and mage all swing for 15. The classes pull apart as soon as you level, because each class grows different attributes.

Gear adds flat attack, defense, and speed, and it can also add a percent of your base attribute. Two pieces do not compound off each other; each percent is taken from the unarmored base.

Classes

Warrior — stamina, STR

Fierce and close. Starts the tree with Power Strike. A shield is the offhand, and it only fits with a one-handed weapon. The shoulder piece is a pauldron.

Archer — stamina, DEX

Precise and ranged. Starts with Quick Shot. A quiver (the data calls the offhand a pouch) is allowed even while a bow or crossbow occupies both hands.

Mage — mana, INT

The only class that spends mana. Starts with Firebolt. An orb or tome needs a one-handed wand. A staff or battle staff occupies both hands, so the focus stays in the bag.

Mage is the resource exception: the bar under your name is mana. Warrior and archer show stamina. Both bars regenerate a little between actions, and some biomes speed that up or slow it down.

Experience

Hero experience and profession experience are separate bars, both capped at 99. Killing a foe grants 16 XP plus 12 per enemy level, plus 15 more for each level they have over you. Draken multiply that reward by 1.05. A profession gains XP from gathering, crafting (usually 5), and enchanting (8), then your lineage percent.
