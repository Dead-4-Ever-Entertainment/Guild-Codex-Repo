Champions

Bosses

Eight hundred named champions, eighty to a landscape, from base level 10 to 100. Dragons are the most common title. A boss does not get the early-level HP bump that ordinary foes get. Their purse is flat gold, not the per-level pocket change.

When you meet one

Every fifth stage, the gold G tile does not end the map. It opens the champion. Beat them and the next stage unlocks. Their written HP, attack, defense, speed, and XP are below; the live fight still scales with your campaign position, your level, and the biome’s danger, up to six times those numbers.

The purse column is 25 gold plus 10 gold for each point of that champion’s number. A low number is a modest payout. A high number is a fortune. That purse replaces the ordinary 10 + 7-per-level coins.

Grassland champions are filed under the plural “grasslands” in the world book. This list shows them as Grassland so they sit with the other landscapes.
