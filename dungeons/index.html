Dungeons

Beyond the wilds between towns, twenty named regions wait. Each is tied to a town and refuses anyone under its hero level. The levels climb far past the opening campaign, into the hundreds.

How they differ from a stage

A campaign stage is a generated floor you reach by leaving town. A dungeon is a named place with a minimum level and a home town. You still move, fight, and gather with the same calls. hero.leave() from a dungeon walks back to the closest town, the same way it does from the wilds.

Two early names share a file id in the atlas (Blighted Lands and Crimson Wastes both point at blighted_lands, and Stormscar’s id is written as Crimson Wastes). Trust the place name and the level, and enter from the town listed beside them.
